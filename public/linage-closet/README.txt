LINAGE CLOSET — WEBSITE PREVIEW

Open index.html in your browser to view the site. For publishing, upload the folder to a static web host (Netlify, Vercel, etc.).

IMPORTANT: This is a storefront prototype, NOT a live e-commerce store.
- Products, prices, and photos are examples only; replace with actual inventory and licensed images.
- Cart works in the browser session; it is not connected to inventory or a database.
- Checkout is a demonstration and does NOT charge cards, submit orders, or store personal details.
- To launch a real store, connect a secure e-commerce platform such as Shopify or WooCommerce with payments, inventory, tax, shipping, order notifications, privacy policies, and return terms.
- Add actual brand contact details, business policies, shipping regions, and product sizes/variants.
- Images and Google Fonts require internet access.
